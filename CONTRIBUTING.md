# Contributing

- **Bugs and suggestions:** open an issue. Please include your PS5 model, firmware, kernel (`uname -r`) and the relevant output.
- **Pull requests** are not accepted. This repo is maintained by one person and changes are made here after testing.
- **Personal customizations** (extra kernel patches, emulator setups, other Gaming Mode changes) are welcome in your own fork. This image stays close to the [ps5-linux](https://github.com/ps5-linux) upstream; kernel patches belong there first.
